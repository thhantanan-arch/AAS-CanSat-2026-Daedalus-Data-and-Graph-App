CFDS Mobile Pro Export State Fix

Issue fixed:
- Export center folder/PNG sections could appear to disappear on iPhone after tapping another expander or download button.

Root cause:
- Streamlit download_button reruns the app by default.
- Expanders can also reset/collapse during reruns, especially on mobile.

Fix:
- Added stable keys to every download button.
- Added on_click="ignore" to download buttons so downloads do not trigger a full rerun.
- Replaced the old export expanders with iPhone-safe selectboxes:
  - Folder ZIP download selector
  - Individual PNG download selector

- streamlit_app.py
